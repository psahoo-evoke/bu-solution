# Your transfer credit at BU: questions and answers

This page explains what you see on your **Student status** page in TransferIQ. It uses plain words. If something here does not match what your advisor or the Registrar tells you, trust them.

> TransferIQ is a prototype built for EvoHack 2026. It runs on made-up (synthetic) students and courses only.

## Who decides my credit?

**BU departments decide. The AI only suggests.**

- Rules that BU already has (grades, credit limits, course types) are applied automatically and the same way for everyone.
- For courses that need a closer look, an AI tool compares your syllabus with BU courses and shows the comparison to a faculty evaluator.
- The evaluator makes the decision. The AI cannot grant or post credit.
- The Registrar then posts the credit to your BU record.

## What do the codes next to my courses mean?

Each course shows a short status in words plus a BU code.

| Code | In plain words | What you need to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **T** | You get credit for this course. It counts as a specific BU course (for example `CAS PS 101`) or as an elective (a BU code ending in `TR`, for example `CAS PS 2TR`). | Nothing. |
| **TX** | No credit. The course is on your record with 0 units. The reason is shown (for example: grade below C, Pass/Fail, one-credit course, remedial course, internship, or a school that is not regionally accredited). | Nothing, unless you think the reason is wrong. Then contact the transfer credit office. |
| **TP** | Pending. Either an evaluator is reviewing it, or it is approved and waiting for the Registrar to post it ("Approved – awaiting posting"). | Nothing. Wait for the decision or the posting. |
| **TS** | Pending: **we need your syllabus** for this course. | Upload the syllabus (see below). |
| **TM** | Pending: **we need more material**. The syllabus we have is incomplete, usually because it has no learning outcomes or no weekly schedule. | Upload a fuller syllabus or the missing pages. |

**"Approved – awaiting posting" (TP)** means the department said yes, but the Registrar has not posted it to your BU record yet. It still counts as **pending**. When it is posted, it changes to **T, "Posted – credit secured"**.

## What is "secured" and what is "pending"?

At the top of your page you see three totals:

- **Secured**: units that are matched from BU's equivalency list or already posted by the Registrar. They count.
- **Pending**: units that are still waiting for a syllabus, more material, a decision or posting. The total says how many of them are already approved and only waiting for posting. Each course also shows how many units it would give you **if approved**.
- **No credit**: courses that do not transfer.

Use the secured total when you plan your first semester. Talk to your advisor before you count on pending units.

## How many units do I get?

- You get the units **you took**, not the units of the BU course. A 5-credit lab course gives 5 units, even if the BU course is 4.
- **Quarter-system** credits are converted: quarter credits × 2/3, rounded to 2 decimals. Example: 4 quarter credits = **2.67** units.
- At most **68 external units** can count toward your BU degree. Units above that are flagged and do not count.
- Your outside grades do **not** go into your BU GPA. Every course that transfers shows grade **T** (or **TX** for no credit).
- Transfer courses do not earn BU Hub units.
- Some majors limit what can come from outside BU (for example, some Computer Science and Economics courses). If this applies to you, a flag on the course says so.

## What should I upload, and where?

1. Open your **Student status** page.
2. **Transcript:** click **Upload transcript (PDF)** at the top.
3. **Syllabus:** find the course with status **TS** or **TM** and click **Upload syllabus** on that row.

What counts as a good syllabus:

- It has **learning outcomes** (what you were expected to learn) **and** a **weekly schedule** of topics.
- A current syllabus from another instructor of the same course (same code and title) is accepted.
- A course description from a catalog is **not** enough. It can give you an early, preliminary suggestion, but an evaluator cannot decide on it.

File rules: PDF only, at most 10 MB and at most 30 pages.

When a complete syllabus arrives, the course moves from **TS** to **TP** (under review).

## Will you remind me?

Yes. If a syllabus is missing, reminders go out on **day 7, day 21 and day 45** by email and in the portal, with a link to upload it.

*(In this prototype the page shows the reminders as scheduled, but email sending is not enabled.)*

## Is there a deadline?

Yes. All your transfer credit must be transferred **within one year of starting at BU**. After that, an **Outstanding Transfer Credit Review** is needed. Upload missing syllabi early.

Courses taken **five or more years** before you start at BU need an extra review before any credit is given.

## What if I took the same course as another student?

When an evaluator approves a course and marks the decision as reusable, the next student with that same course gets the match instantly. You do not need to send that syllabus again. Your course shows "Approved – awaiting posting" until the Registrar posts your record.

## Who sees my information?

Only staff roles who need it: your advisor (read-only), evaluators, the transfer credit office and the Registrar. Your name and student ID are **never** sent to the AI tool. The AI only sees course information (title, description, outcomes, topics and syllabus).
