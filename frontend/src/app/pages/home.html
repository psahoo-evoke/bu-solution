How it works

  1. 1
    Upload

    The student or credit staff uploads the transcript; syllabi are added as they come in.

  2. 2
    Pre-match

    Known equivalencies are secured straight away; the AI compares the rest with the BU catalog and past decisions.

  3. 3
    Decide

    The department reviews the evidence and decides; strong matches can be approved as a batch.

  4. 4
    Post

    The registrar posts approved credit and exports it for MyBU. Every step is on the audit trail.
